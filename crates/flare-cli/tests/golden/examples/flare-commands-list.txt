PATH                  ABOUT
flare                 Serve a board's pages and bindings on this machine, open them in a browser, publish pages and save bindings, and share files through a flare server
flare serve           Serve a board's pages and bindings, locally or behind a TLS proxy, until Ctrl-C
flare open            Open the running server's index, a page or a binding in a browser
flare publish         Publish a page version from an HTML or Markdown file
flare bind            Save a binding that fills a page's slots
flare login           Sign in to a flare server and keep its token in the Keychain
flare logout          Revoke and delete this computer's token
flare share           Share a Markdown file
flare show            Show one version of a share you can open, or its latest
flare grant           Grant a share to readers
flare revoke          End grants on a share
flare grants          List grants
flare teams           Manage teams
flare teams create    Create a team that you own, with you as its first member
flare teams add       Add people to a team you own
flare teams remove    Remove people from a team you own, or leave a team you belong to
flare teams list      List the teams you belong to
flare teams show      Show the members of teams you belong to
flare teams transfer  Hand a team you own to one of its members, who becomes its owner
flare teams delete    Delete a team you own, removing its grant from every flare and freeing its name
flare shares          List your shares or those granted to you
flare activity        List responses and comments since a cursor
flare comments        List a flare's comments and replies
flare comment         Comment on a flare
flare reply           Reply to a comment
