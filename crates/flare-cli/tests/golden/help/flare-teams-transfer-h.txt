Hand a team you own to one of its members, who becomes its owner

Usage: flare teams transfer [OPTIONS] --to <PERSON> <NAME>

Arguments:
  <NAME>  The team's name

Options:
      --to <PERSON>  The member who becomes the owner: an address, or a name the server resolves
      --yes          Proceed without asking for confirmation
  -h, --help         Print help (see more with '--help')

Examples:
  flare teams transfer design --to jo@example.org --yes

Effects: destructive, idempotent, closed-world.
Global flags: --json, --fields <FIELD>, --long, --full, --no-input. `flare teams transfer --help` describes them.
