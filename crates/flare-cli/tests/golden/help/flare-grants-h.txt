List a share's grants

Usage: flare grants [OPTIONS] <ID>

Arguments:
  <ID>  The share whose grants to list; you must own it or hold Admin on it

Options:
  -h, --help  Print help (see more with '--help')

Examples:
  flare grants 3f9c0a17e2b44d6f8a1c5b7e9d20f4a6

Effects: read-only, closed-world.
Global flags: --json, --fields <FIELD>, --long, --full, --no-input. `flare grants --help` describes them.
Next: flare grants --help explains each column.
