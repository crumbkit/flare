Manage the teams that shares are granted to

Usage: flare teams [OPTIONS] <COMMAND>

Commands:
  create  Create a team that you own, with you as its first member
  add     Add people to a team you own
  remove  Remove people from a team you own, or leave a team you belong to
  list    List the teams you belong to
  show    Show the members of teams you belong to
  delete  Delete a team you own; its grants stay and open nothing, and its name is never used again

Options:
      --json
          Print the result as a JSON document. The default prints text.

      --fields <FIELD>
          Print only these comma-separated fields, in this order. The default prints the compact columns.

      --long
          Add the long columns after the compact ones. The default prints only the compact columns.

      --full
          With --json, print every field of every row. The default prints only the fields of the columns.

      --no-input
          Fail instead of asking a question

  -h, --help
          Print help (see a summary with '-h')

Next: flare teams <COMMAND> -h
