Hand a team you own to one of its members, who becomes its owner

Usage: flare teams transfer [OPTIONS] --to <PERSON> <NAME>

Arguments:
  <NAME>
          The team's name

Options:
      --to <PERSON>
          The member who becomes the owner: an address, or a name the server resolves

      --yes
          Proceed without asking for confirmation

      --json
          Print the result as a JSON document. The default prints text.

      --fields <FIELD>
          Print only these comma-separated fields, in this order. The default prints the compact columns.

      --long
          Add the long columns after the compact ones. The default prints only the compact columns.

      --full
          With --json, print every field of every row. The default prints only the fields of the columns.

      --no-input
          Fail instead of asking a question

  -h, --help
          Print help (see a summary with '-h')

Examples:
  flare teams transfer design --to jo@example.org --yes
          Hand the team design to its member jo@example.org

Columns:
  name        The team's name.
  owner       The owner's identifier; none for a team that your organisation's directory keeps.
  managed_by  Who keeps the team: `flare`, or `directory` for a team that your organisation's directory keeps and flare cannot change.
  role        What you are to the team: `owner` or `member`.
  members     How many members the team has, its owner included.

Columns that --long adds:
  created  When flare created the team, in RFC 3339 UTC; none for a directory team.

Effects: destructive, idempotent, closed-world.
