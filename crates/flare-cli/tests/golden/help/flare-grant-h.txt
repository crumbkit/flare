Grant a share to readers

Usage: flare grant [OPTIONS] <ID>

Arguments:
  <ID>  The share to grant

Options:
      --with <WHO>  Who to grant Reader, repeated or separated by commas: an address, team:<name> for a team you belong to, or everyone
      --everyone    Grant Reader to everyone signed in whom the server's sign-in rule covers; only the share's owner may
      --re <ID>     The flares the share responds to, repeated or separated by commas; only its owner may add them, and the flares' owners become Readers of it
  -h, --help        Print help (see more with '--help')

Examples:
  flare grant 3f9c0a17e2b44d6f8a1c5b7e9d20f4a6 --with mei@example.org
  flare grant 3f9c0a17e2b44d6f8a1c5b7e9d20f4a6 --with team:design
  flare grant 3f9c0a17e2b44d6f8a1c5b7e9d20f4a6 --everyone

Effects: additive, not idempotent, closed-world.
Global flags: --json, --fields <FIELD>, --full, --no-input. `flare grant --help` describes them.
