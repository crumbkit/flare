Grant an existing share to more people

Usage: flare grant [OPTIONS] <ID>

Arguments:
  <ID>  The share to grant

Options:
      --with <ADDRESS>  The addresses to grant Reader, repeated or separated by commas
      --re <ID>         The flares the share responds to, repeated or separated by commas; only its owner may add them
  -h, --help            Print help (see more with '--help')

Examples:
  flare grant 3f9c0a17e2b44d6f8a1c5b7e9d20f4a6 --with mei@example.org

Effects: additive, not idempotent, closed-world.
Global flags: --json, --fields <FIELD>, --full, --no-input. `flare grant --help` describes them.
