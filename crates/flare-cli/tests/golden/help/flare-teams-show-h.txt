Show the members of teams you belong to

Usage: flare teams show [OPTIONS] <NAME>...

Arguments:
  <NAME>...  The teams' names, up to 100

Options:
  -h, --help  Print help (see more with '--help')

Examples:
  flare teams show design

Effects: read-only, closed-world.
Global flags: --json, --fields <FIELD>, --full, --no-input. `flare teams show --help` describes them.
