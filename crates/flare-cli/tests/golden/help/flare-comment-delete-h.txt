Delete a comment or reply you wrote

Usage: flare comment-delete [OPTIONS] <COMMENT>

Arguments:
  <COMMENT>  The comment or reply to delete, which only its author may

Options:
      --yes   Proceed without asking for confirmation
  -h, --help  Print help (see more with '--help')

Examples:
  flare comment-delete 8d1f0c2a9b7e4c53a6f20e1d4b9c7a85 --yes

Effects: destructive, not idempotent, closed-world.
Global flags: --json, --fields <FIELD>, --full, --no-input. `flare comment-delete --help` describes them.
