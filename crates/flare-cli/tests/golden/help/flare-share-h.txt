Share a Markdown file with people, or as a response to flares

Usage: flare share [OPTIONS] <FILE>

Arguments:
  <FILE>  The file to share: .md for Markdown

Options:
      --title <TITLE>   The share's title; without it, the file's name without its ending
      --with <ADDRESS>  The addresses to grant Reader, repeated or separated by commas
      --re <ID>         The flares this share responds to, repeated or separated by commas
  -h, --help            Print help (see more with '--help')

Examples:
  flare share review.md --with ana@example.org,jo@example.org
  flare share answer.md --re 3f9c0a17e2b44d6f8a1c5b7e9d20f4a6

Effects: additive, not idempotent, closed-world.
Global flags: --json, --fields <FIELD>, --full, --no-input. `flare share --help` describes them.
