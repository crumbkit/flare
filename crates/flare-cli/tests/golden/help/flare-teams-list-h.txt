List the teams you belong to

Usage: flare teams list [OPTIONS]

Options:
      --limit <N>  Print at most this many rows, up to 100 [default: 50]
      --all        Print every row
  -h, --help       Print help (see more with '--help')

Examples:
  flare teams list

Effects: read-only, closed-world.
Global flags: --json, --fields <FIELD>, --long, --full, --no-input. `flare teams list --help` describes them.
