Manage teams

Usage: flare teams [OPTIONS] <COMMAND>

Commands:
  create    Create a team that you own, with you as its first member
  add       Add people to a team you own
  remove    Remove people from a team you own, or leave a team you belong to
  list      List the teams you belong to
  show      Show the members of teams you belong to
  transfer  Hand a team you own to one of its members, who becomes its owner
  delete    Delete a team you own, removing its grant from every flare and freeing its name

Options:
  -h, --help  Print help (see more with '--help')

Global flags: --json, --fields <FIELD>, --long, --full, --no-input. `flare teams --help` describes them.
