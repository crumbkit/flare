Remove people from a team you own, or leave a team you belong to

Usage: flare teams remove [OPTIONS] --with <PERSON> <NAME>

Arguments:
  <NAME>  The team's name

Options:
      --with <PERSON>  The people, repeated or separated by commas: each an address, or a name the server resolves
      --yes            Proceed without asking for confirmation
  -h, --help           Print help (see more with '--help')

Examples:
  flare teams remove design --with jo@example.org

Effects: destructive, not idempotent, closed-world.
Global flags: --json, --fields <FIELD>, --full, --no-input. `flare teams remove --help` describes them.
Next: flare teams remove --help explains each column.
