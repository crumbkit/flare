End grants on a share

Usage: flare revoke [OPTIONS] <--with <WHO>|--everyone> <ID>

Arguments:
  <ID>  The share whose grants end

Options:
      --with <WHO>  Whose grant ends, repeated or separated by commas: an address, team:<name> or everyone
      --everyone    End the grant to everyone signed in; only the share's owner may
      --yes         Proceed without asking for confirmation
  -h, --help        Print help (see more with '--help')

Examples:
  flare revoke 3f9c0a17e2b44d6f8a1c5b7e9d20f4a6 --with mei@example.org --yes
  flare revoke 3f9c0a17e2b44d6f8a1c5b7e9d20f4a6 --everyone --yes

Effects: destructive, not idempotent, closed-world.
Global flags: --json, --fields <FIELD>, --full, --no-input. `flare revoke --help` describes them.
